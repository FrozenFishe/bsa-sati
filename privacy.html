Privacy Policy

Sati: Meditate & Study · Last updated 17 October 2026

In short: Sati collects no personal data. Everything you record stays on your device. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

Who we are

Sati is offered free of charge by the Buddhist Society of Australia, a charity registered with the Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission (ABN 35 892 665 974), based in Sydney, New South Wales.

What Sati stores, and where

Your goals, meditation and study minutes, lesson progress, check-in figures (screen time, pickups and app minutes you enter), guards and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or using Settings › Erase all data, removes them.

Apple Health

If you choose to allow it, Sati writes your completed meditation sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. Sati does not read any other Health data, and Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Screen Time

If you allow Screen Time access, Sati uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks to show your usage and to apply the guards you set. That information is processed on your device by Apple’s frameworks and is never sent to us.

Notifications

Practice reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push-notification service or server is used.

Things we do not do

We do not collect names, emails, locations, contacts, identifiers or usage analytics. We do not sell or share data, use advertising, or track you across apps and websites.

Links

Sati links to the Society’s website, our giving page (processed by Zeffy) and the ACNC register. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Sati is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: info@buddhistsocietiesofaustralia.org.

# REA findings: Brainrot reference

Tool: `rea-agents@6.5.0`, CDP website observation (`list-browser-targets`, `inspect-web-page --include-accessibility-text`)
against the public ScreensDesign showcase page for *Brainrot: Screen Time Control* (2026-10-17).
Evidence ID: `ev_` captured locally. The raw capture is not committed because it contains third-party copyrighted text.

Observed product patterns, paraphrased:
- Onboarding of ~28 steps, ending in a hard paywall with a yearly or weekly plan and a time-limited discount pop-up.
- An interactive screen-time slider where the mascot decays as hours increase.
- A quiz that produces a named "attention profile".
- A projection of lifetime years lost to the phone, followed by years that could be regained.
- Home screen with a health score, a calendar of daily emoji, and a home-screen widget.
- From the App Store listing: blocking via the Screen Time API, a focus/Pomodoro timer, schedules, an intervention screen with 5/10/15/30-minute options, streaks, and unlockable themes.

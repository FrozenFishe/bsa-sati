Common questions

Is Sati free? Yes. It has no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Sati is offered by the Buddhist Society of Australia, a registered charity.

Where is my data? Only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

How does Padma’s clarity score work? It rises with meditation, study, lessons and pauses where you chose presence, and falls when screen time goes over your goal.

What does a Guard do? During the times you set, it makes your mindful pause stricter for the apps on your list. With Screen Time permission, Sati can also shield those apps.

How do I reset? Settings › Erase all data.
